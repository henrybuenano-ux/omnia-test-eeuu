Privacy Policy

VN Supply Kitchen Cabinets · Effective date: July 13, 2026

VN Supply Kitchen Cabinets (“VN Supply,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website vnskcb.com and provides kitchen cabinet sales and installation services in Miami and West Palm Beach, Florida. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request an estimate, or communicate with us by phone, text message (SMS), WhatsApp, email, or social media.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: name, phone number, email address, and mailing/installation address.
  • Project information: measurements, photos of your space, product preferences, budget range, and notes you share with us to prepare your estimate.
  • Business information: if you are a contractor, handyman, or showroom, your company name and business details.
  • Communications: messages you exchange with us via web forms, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media.
  • Website usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and similar analytics collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and prepare estimates.
  • To schedule visits, measurements, deliveries, and installations, and send you related confirmations and reminders.
  • To process orders and payments and keep records of your project.
  • To follow up on estimates and, with your consent, send helpful information about our products and services.
  • To request your feedback after a completed project.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

By providing your phone number through our website forms, chat, or in conversation with our team, you consent to receive text messages (SMS) and/or WhatsApp messages from VN Supply Kitchen Cabinets related to your inquiry, estimates, appointment confirmations and reminders, order and installation updates, and occasional service information.

  • Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will confirm your unsubscribe status and no further messages will be sent.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at info@vnskcb.com or +1 (786) 444-1228.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent collected for text messaging are used solely by VN Supply Kitchen Cabinets to communicate with you and will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third party for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding service providers acting solely on our behalf (such as our messaging platform).

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate our business (e.g., our customer relationship management platform, messaging and email providers, and payment processor), strictly to perform services on our behalf.
  • Installation and delivery personnel as needed to complete your project.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use our site (including advertising analytics from platforms such as Meta). You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain your information for as long as needed to serve you, maintain business records, and meet legal requirements. We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw your communication consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. Residents of certain states may have additional rights under applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
